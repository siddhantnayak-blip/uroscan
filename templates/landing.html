How it works

1. Take a photo→ 2. KNN matches each pad's colour→ 3. Saved in MySQL→ 4. Report, AI summary & graph
🎨

Colour matching

Each pad's colour is compared with the colour chart and the closest match is picked (K-nearest neighbour).

🗄️

Stored safely

Reports are saved in a MySQL database in one transaction. A trigger marks each result Normal or High.

🩺

Doctor notes

Doctors pick a patient, open their reports and add notes the patient can read.
