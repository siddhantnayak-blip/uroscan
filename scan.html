Tips for a good reading

  1. Dip the strip, then wait about 60 seconds (use the timer)
  2. Lay it on a plain white card or paper
  3. Tip (first pad) on the left, handle on the right
  4. Even daylight or room light, no flash, no shadows
  5. Keep all 10 pads in the photo
